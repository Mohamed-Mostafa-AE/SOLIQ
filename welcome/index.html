THE PROBLEM

THE NEXT CROP
ISN’T A GUESS.

Rainfall shifts. Heat builds. Water is limited. A crop that protects one field may be impractical in another because of planting dates, equipment, soil, or market access.

Farmers need a way to compare their existing rotation with alternatives using evidence they can inspect and local constraints they can actually confirm.

OUR APPROACH

FROM ORBIT
TO THE FIELD.

Soliq combines NASA POWER weather available in 2026, historical weather from 2016–2025, declared soil-water conditions, crop characteristics, and farmer priorities.

It compares your editable current calendar with two alternatives using declared soil evidence, practical constraints and your priorities. Water demand, planned fallow windows and daily modeled storage make the trade-offs visible.

Missing local evidence stays visible. The closest historical weather years supply conditional scenarios for the remainder of 2026. Kimi Pro and DeepSeek can explain these results. The prototype is a decision aid; its crop outcomes still need field validation.
