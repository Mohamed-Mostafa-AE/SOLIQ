02 / OPEN QUESTIONS, VISIBLE EVIDENCE

How much evidence
does a decision need?

Our proposed research asks whether tracking decision reversals—and directing the next measurement toward the uncertainty that matters—can improve crop-rotation decisions under limited data.

The current prototype compares four deterministic water scenarios. Measurement selection, satellite fusion, and field validation remain research work.
