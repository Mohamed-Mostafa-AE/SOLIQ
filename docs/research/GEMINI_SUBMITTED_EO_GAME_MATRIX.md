## 2. Local Agronomy, Water Accounting & Model Boundaries

1. **Daily Effective Soil-Bucket Model (`Manuscript Section V.B, Eq. 4–8`)**:
   - Operates in a **non-flooded, fixed-depth root-zone bucket domain** with capacity $S_{\max} > 0\text{ mm}$:
     $$A_t = S_t + P_t - Q_t + I^{\text{eff}}_t, \quad L_t = \max(0, A_t - S_{\max})$$
     $$ET^*_t = (K_{s,t} K_{cb,t} + K_{e,t}) ET_{0,t}, \quad ET_t = \min(ET^*_t, A_t - L_t), \quad S_{t+1} = A_t - L_t - ET_t$$
   - **Process-Order & Mass-Conservation Verification**: As proven on Manuscript Page 5, for $S_t = 90\text{ mm}, P_t = 30\text{ mm}, Q_t = I^{\text{eff}}_t = 0, S_{\max} = 100\text{ mm}, ET^*_t = 5\text{ mm}$, the drainage-first order (`Eq. 4–8`) drains $L_t = 20\text{ mm}$ and ends at $S_{t+1} = 95\text{ mm}$ (whereas an ET-first balance drains $15\text{ mm}$ and ends at $100\text{ mm}$), with daily closure residual $|S_{t+1} - S_t - (P_t - Q_t + I^{\text{eff}}_t - L_t - ET_t)| \le 10^{-6}\text{ mm}$.
   - **Algebraic Irrigation Non-Uniqueness (`Manuscript Section VI.E, Eq. 18 & Fig. 2`)**: In $\Delta S = P + I_{\text{eff}} + C - ET - Q - L$, observing $P = Q = \Delta S = 0$ and $ET = 40\text{ mm}$ constrains only $I_{\text{eff}} + C - L = 40\text{ mm}$; the triples $(I_{\text{eff}}, C, L) = (40, 0, 0)$, $(60, 0, 20)$, and $(25, 15, 0)$ all yield identical $\Delta S$ and $ET$. Therefore, satellite ET and soil moisture cannot uniquely recover field irrigation delivery without management records or process constraints.
2. **Sourced Regional Crop & Salinity Boundaries**:
   - **Taxonomic Precision**: Egyptian Berseem clover is `Trifolium alexandrinum` ([FAO Paper x7660e](https://www.fao.org/4/x7660e/x7660e08.htm)), strictly distinct from perennial Alfalfa (`Medicago sativa`) used in the Diamond Valley, Nevada configuration.
   - **Salinity Thresholds (`FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 29 Rev. 1`)**: Referenced to [FAO-29 `t0234e`](https://www.fao.org/4/t0234e/t0234e00.htm) (`f2430e` is Paper 24). The winter crossover $EC_e^* = 2.878235653\text{ dS/m}$ ($1.6 + \frac{6700}{54600 \times 0.096}$) is explicitly labeled as a **conditional fixture calculation**, never a universal field constant.
   - **Subsurface Drainage & Gypsum Boundary**: Referenced to [NWRC & HR Wallingford Report OD/TN 96](https://eprints.hrwallingford.com/id/eprint/417/1/odtn96.pdf). When water-table depth $< 60\text{ cm}$ or $\text{ESP} > 15\%$, the model triggers a **conditional diagnostic warning (`Drainage Safety Gate`)** recommending physical inspection of subsurface tile drains before applying leaching water; gypsum quantities are educational screening estimates requiring laboratory CEC, bulk density, and soil depth measurements.
