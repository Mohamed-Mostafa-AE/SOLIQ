## English text to paste in the NASA project submission
**Title:** SOLIQ — Evidence-Aware Crop-Rotation Exploration

**Short summary:** SOLIQ helps farmers and advisers explore the trade-offs of crop-rotation choices using dated NASA weather context, explicit local assumptions, water accounting and transparent uncertainty — without presenting simulated outcomes as field-validated results.

**Description:** Seasonal crop choices affect irrigation timing, soil-cover opportunities and practical constraints. SOLIQ is a farmer-facing prototype that compares existing and candidate planting calendars under a shared water/weather context. Its current working features include NASA POWER regional daily weather, crop screening, reproducible numerical water accounting, bilingual explanations and a separate interactive six-farm water cooperation prototype. The FieldShift methods draft proposes a conservative interval-regret gate for not preferring options under unresolved uncertainty; its reported numerical experiment is synthetic. The project does not yet claim independent farm-level validation, yield or monetary improvements, real SAR soil-salinity retrieval, or certified local crop advice. We are working on field geometry/soil validation, actual optical/precipitation data products, SAR QA and independent farmer/agronomist evaluation.

**Data / references:** NASA POWER Daily gridded meteorology powers the implemented weather pathway; source units, date ranges and coverage are shown. GPM IMERG, NASA/USGS HLS and ASF NISAR/Sentinel-1 are planned/partial connectors, not currently validated pixel-based results.

**AI usage:** Credit OpenAI ChatGPT/Codex and Google Gemini/Antigravity for their actual contributions, plus other models if used. Human team authors approve claims. Cite each external source/asset and license.
