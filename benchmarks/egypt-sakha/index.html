Weather prediction benchmark

Four retrospective chronological holdouts (2022–2025). Every target year uses earlier training years only. The target is remaining-year rainfall after the November 12 cutoff.

MethodRainfall mean absolute error
Five historical analogs20.32 mm

The analog method performs worse than climatology in these four tests. This benchmark demonstrates no rainfall-prediction advantage. Both methods have zero heat-day error because all four held-out late-year periods contain zero days at or above 35 °C; this trivial result does not establish heat-prediction skill.

Limits that matter here

No independent 2026 station, soil or irrigation measurements were available. The cited Sakha research describes both saline and non-saline soils; those old measurements are not assumed to describe this point today. Flooded rice, salinity and groundwater processes are outside this model's scope. Quantitative crop suitability, yield, profits and soil-health benefits are not validated.

The AI demo uses an actual open embedding model to retrieve reviewed agronomy guidance. Farm-report numbers come from the NASA-backed model. Neural retrieval tests and a five-question source-relevance diagnostic are retained in the GitHub browser artifact; they do not establish agronomic accuracy.
